# Case Study: Optimizing Pre-Planning Sales Performance for Nirvana Asia
By: DHaVoC1301 | Data Analyst Portfolio Project

## 📌 1. Business Problem & Context
As a sales agent for Nirvana Asia, tracking client demographics and operational closing speed is critical to maximizing agency revenue. Because actual customer records are strictly confidential, I synthesized a realistic 200-row operational dataset using Python to model sales parameters including burial lands, columbarium niches, and pre-planned funeral packages.

The goal of this analysis is to identify top revenue drivers, understand target customer age profiles, and uncover bottlenecks in sales closing timelines.

## 🛠️ 2. Tech Stack & Methodology
* **Data Synthesis:** Python (Randomization logic matching local product pricing structures)
* **Analysis & Modeling:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced Pivot Tables, Data Formatting)
* **Interactivity:** Linked Excel Slicers & Dynamic Dashboard Visualizations

## 📊 3. Key Business Insights
Through dynamic multi-table pivot processing, the following operational realities were identified from the dataset:

* **Demographics:** The average age of a premium Burial Land buyer is **66** years old, representing the oldest consumer tier. Columbarium Niche buyers average **58** years old, while Funeral Packages capture the youngest audience at an average of **50** years old.
* **Financial Drivers:** **Burial Land** serves as the agency's primary revenue driver, contributing a total of **RM 2,353,351.00** to sales. The most lucrative asset sectors are heavily concentrated within **Zone A**.
* **Sales Efficiency:** Contracts settled via Cash are highly efficient, closing in an average of **16** days from initial client inquiry. In contrast, agreements relying on Installment financing suffer from severe operational drag, taking an average of **63** days to finalize. 
* **The Closing Gap:** Processing installment agreements introduces a net operational delay of **47** days to the sales lifecycle.

## 💡 4. Strategic Business Recommendations
Based on the dashboard insights, I recommend implementing the following three data-driven corporate actions:

1. **Age-Segmented Marketing Funnels:** Restructure localized promotional campaigns to target premium burial plots exclusively to prospects aged 55+. Position columbarium niches and entry-level pre-planning funeral packages as starter security solutions tailored for consumers in their late 40s and early 50s.
2. **Inventory Management:** Allocate higher promotion budgets and agent incentives toward **Zone A** assets, as data confirms this sector commands the strongest velocity of capital return and consumer demand.
3. **Cash Conversion Incentives:** To mitigate the **47-day** bottleneck identified in financing credit processing, introduce a **3% upfront cash incentive discount**. This targeted price adjustment will encourage cash settlements, accelerate agency cash flow, and optimize corporate liquidity.
